How does it work?

Validate an ISBN or B-prefixed Amazon ASIN, reconcile available public catalog evidence, then enrich the author and video opportunity with a reviewable audit trail.

  • Checksum agreement: ISBNlib, pyisbn, and the built-in verifier must agree before an ISBN enters the pipeline.
  • Public catalog reconciliation: Exact identifier matches from Google Books, Open Library, and the Library of Congress are compared field by field. Conflicts remain visible for review.
  • Audited enrichment: Public author, contact, social, and video signals retain source links, confidence, warnings, and human-review status.
Free-first, not unlimited: no-key providers are still subject to fair-use limits and availability. Cached evidence reduces repeated requests.
