How does it work?

Validate an ISBN or B-prefixed Amazon ASIN, reconcile available public catalog evidence, then enrich the author and video opportunity with a reviewable audit trail.

  • Checksum agreement ISBNlib, pyisbn, and the built-in verifier must agree before an ISBN enters the pipeline.
  • Public catalog reconciliation Exact identifier matches from Google Books, Open Library, and the Library of Congress are compared field by field.
  • Audited enrichment Public author, contact, social, and video signals retain source links, confidence score, and human-review status.
Fair Use & Caching: Public discovery queries are rate-limited and cached to prevent repeated requests.
